Escape from War Zone

Guide your little sister across a broken country to the last ships. Hide, keep moving, and never let go of her hand.

Testing now, coming soon to Google Play

The story

War has come to the country of Vestara. On the first morning, sixteen-year-old Kiaan and his seven-year-old sister Mira lose their parents in the chaos. Their father has one instruction: get to Port Salvia, where the last ships leave.

You play as Kiaan. Mira follows one step behind you. If you run ahead and leave her, she sits down and cries, and the patrols hear her. You cannot leave her behind.

How you play

There is no fighting. You hide in doorways, behind rubble, in bushes and inside burnt-out cars, and move when the patrols look away. Hold Mira's hand to run together, lift her over walls and pull her up onto rooftops, and pick up bread and water on the way. Campfires are where you rest.

Every chapter adds one new danger: searchlights in the dark, a bridge that crumbles under your feet, guard dogs that hear every step.

The journey

Eight chapters, one country, from the city where it began to the sea. Each chapter takes about 5 to 10 minutes.

  1. The Morning Everything EndedYour home city, the first morning. Learn to walk, hide and hold Mira's hand.
  2. Ghost StreetsEmpty city blocks and patrols. Move only when their backs are turned.
  3. Lights in the DarkNight falls, and searchlights sweep the streets.
  4. The Broken BridgeA great bridge over the river, falling apart under your feet.
  5. The Silent ForestGuard dogs cannot see well, but they hear everything.
  6. The CampA place to rest, with people, stories and food.
  7. The BorderFences, towers and drones. The hardest crossing.
  8. The Last KilometerThe road to Port Salvia at sunrise.
