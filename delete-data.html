Delete your data

This page explains how to delete your data from Escape from War Zone and our other games, made by Emberkite Games.

In short: our games have no accounts and we keep no data about you on our own servers. Your game progress lives only on your phone, so you can delete it yourself at any time. For anything else, email us and we will delete it.

1. Your game progress and settings

Saved only on your phone (chapters unlocked, sound, graphics and control settings). We never receive them. To delete them, either:

  • uninstall the game, or
  • open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then Escape from War Zone, then Storage, and tap Clear data.

This deletes everything at once and cannot be undone.

2. Advertising data

Ads in our games come from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID (see our privacy policy). To delete or reset that ID, open your phone's Settings, then Privacy, then Ads (on some phones: Settings, then Google, then Ads), and tap Delete advertising ID or Reset advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they are no longer linked to the old ID.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you can also withdraw your consent to personalised ads in the game's Settings, under Privacy options. Data that Google holds is kept and deleted under Google's privacy policy; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com.

3. Emails you sent us, and the tester group

To have your emails to us deleted, email emberkitegames@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and tell us which address you wrote from. We delete them within 30 days and reply to confirm. We keep nothing else about you.

If you joined our tester group, leaving the Emberkite Testers group removes your address from it straight away.

Questions

Email emberkitegames@gmail.com and we will help.
